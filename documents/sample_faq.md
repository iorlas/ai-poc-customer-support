## Return Policy

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. Items must be:
- In original packaging
- Unused and in new condition
- With proof of purchase

To initiate a return:
1. Contact our support team
2. Receive a return authorization number (RMA)
3. Ship the item back with the RMA number
4. Refund processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the item

Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can also track your order by:
1. Logging into your account
2. Going to "Order History"
3. Clicking on the order number
4. Viewing tracking information

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do you offer gift wrapping?**
A: Yes! Gift wrapping is available for $4.99 per item during checkout.

**Q: Can I change my order after placing it?**
A: Orders can be modified within 1 hour of placement. After that, the order enters processing and cannot be changed.

**Q: Do you price match?**
A: Yes, we offer price matching on identical items from authorized retailers. Contact us with proof of the lower price.

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**
A: Absolutely. We use industry-standard SSL encryption and do not store your complete payment information.
