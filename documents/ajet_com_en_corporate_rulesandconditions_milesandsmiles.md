## Miles&Smiles

**AJet – Miles&Smiles Partnership Rules and Conditions**

Miles&Smiles program

[terms and conditions](https://www.turkishairlines.com/en-tr/miles-and-smiles/terms-and-conditions/index.html)apply.Miles&Smiles members can earn Miles and Status Miles from paid and scheduled AJet flights purchased as of June 30, 2025.

Miles&Smiles members can earn Miles and Status Miles on paid, scheduled domestic and international flights operated by AJet.

The partner airline reserves the right to change eligible destinations with prior notice.

Miles accrual is based on the booking classes defined by the operating airline.

Missing Miles (retro claim) requests for AJet flights can be submitted no earlier than the

__8th day after the flight and up to 12 months afterward__.Retro claims for AJet flights can be submitted online. Members can use the “My Miles Transactions” section on the website or mobile app after logging in, or submit a request via the

[feedback form](https://feedback.turkishairlines.com/en)on the website.Miles&Smiles members cannot use status benefits on AJet flights other than access to certain designated lounges. For details on eligible lounges and usage rules, please

[click here.](https://www.turkishairlines.com/en-int/any-questions/lounge/terms-and-conditions/index.html)Award tickets cannot be issued for AJet flights.

Miles&Smiles members should keep original boarding passes and copies of tickets until the flight information appears in their account summary.
