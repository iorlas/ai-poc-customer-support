# Meal Selection

Get ready for an enjoyable journey with AJet’s gourmet flavors!

On all international flights with AJet, you can use the “Meal Selection” service for a fee to make your journey more enjoyable.

Our carefully prepared meal menu will accompany you during your journey with the quality of Turkish DO&CO.

Your journey will turn into an unforgettable taste experience with a breakfast menu for a good start to the day, cold sandwiches and wraps for those who want a quick and satisfying snack, carefully prepared main menu options, and traditional Turkish desserts. Our children’s menu is prepared for our little guests, and our special vegetarian menu for passengers who care about their dietary habits are among the options you can choose for your journey.

You can review the orders that can be ordered from AJet Cafe on our domestic flights and the price list from our [menu.](https://cdn.cloud.ajet.com/prod/others/services_meal_selection_ajet_cafe_domestic_menu_november_24_2025WEBOUTSIDE_EN.pdf)

You can review the orders that can be ordered from AJet Cafe on our international flights and the price list from our [menu.](https://cms-ajet-prod.s3.amazonaws.com/others/AJet_Cafe_International_Menu.pdf_EN.pdf)

You can review the meals available for order and their prices from our [menu](https://cdn.cloud.ajet.com/prod/others/ajet_international_pre-order_menuWEBOUTSIDE_EN.pdf) for international flights.

You can review the content, calories, and allergen information of our menus from [here](https://cdn.cloud.ajet.com/others/AJet_Alerjen_Bilgi.pdf).

You can make your meal selection at any time during and after ticketing up to 24 hours before your flight.

You can review the [Rules and Conditions](https://ajet.com/en/corporate/rules-and-conditions/meal-selection) and [Frequently Asked Questions](https://ajet.com/en/frequently-asked-questions#Meal%20Selection) menus related to meal selection.
