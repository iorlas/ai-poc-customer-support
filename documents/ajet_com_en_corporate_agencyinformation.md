## Agency Information

**1. AJet Booking and Ticketing Regulations**

AJet aims to ensure that travel service providers (agencies) are protected from penalties and sanctions imposed by airlines by setting the boundaries of the appropriate use of reservation and ticketing rules which are binding for all agencies, authorized or unauthorized, with or without IATA accreditation, that are using systems subject to GDS and AJET direct sales channels.

The agency’s responsibility commences as soon as it accesses AJet’ reservation system. The rules regarding the reservation and ticketing operations performed by agencies are specified in detail in the IATA directives numbered 830a, 830d, 824, 850m. In this respect, IATA agencies must perform reservation and ticketing operations in accordance with the IATA General Rules as well as the fees, announcements, procedures and regulations published by AJet in accordance with the applicable transport rules; non-IATA agencies must perform reservation and ticketing operations in accordance with the fees, announcements, procedures and regulations published by AJet in accordance with the applicable transport rules.

We urge all Travel Service Provider reservation staff to strictly follow procedures and regrettably non-compliance of these rules will result with debit to the service provider. Travel Service Provider will also be prohibited from selling AJet inventory. Rules which are related to reservations and ticketing done by the agencies indicated in 830a, 830d, 824, 850m instructions of IATA with all details. Agencies have to conduct ticketing according to IATA General Rules and fares, notifications and regulations published by AJet.

AJet holds the right to apply changes to its Debit Memo/Booking policy without notifying beforehand therefore it is under the responsibility of Travel Service Provider to update its employees on the regulations. AJet can apply below mentioned ADM amounts to all booking practices which are not applicable rules and workflow.

It is advisable to follow the below mentioned rules to maintain a good level of service for all business partners.

**DEFINITIONS**

Inactive Booking: Segments in PNR with status code PN, HX, UN, NO, SC, TK, UC, US or WK.

Group Booking: A booking made for 10 or more passengers in a PNR.

Duplicate Booking: A booking for the same passenger on the same/different route for the same/similar dates.

Customer Tracking and Testing Booking: A booking created for testing, employee training, or customer tracking (itinerary or invoice printing).

No-Show: A booking where the passenger does not board the flight although the booking has been confirmed or the ticket is issued.

Passive Booking: Non-active booking entered in the GDS to issue ticket for active booking originally hosted in the airline system.

Travel Service Provider: Agency or organization providing travel service.

Speculative / Fictitious Booking: A booking created by using fake names for passengers who do not actually exist, for different purposes such as filling the sales quota

Churning: Segments that are repeatedly cancelled and rebooked to circumvent time limits or to meet GDS productivity.

**2. Reservation Rules for Agencies**

**2.1 Speculative /Fictitious Bookings**

An ADM is issued /**25.-EUR **flat fee to all booking practices listed in below will be collected.

Post departure bookings.

Bookings made with fake names. Not limited to but such as surnames ABC/FGHJK/. Initials like A/B/C, names of celebrities or test names

Impossible Bookings : Itinerary with bookings illogical for passenger to meet such as multiple destinations, bookings with connections that depart before arrival of the inbound flight.

Booking created to block the space or to reach the GDS designated productivity count, if there is no definite passenger.

Open segments entered for other that of ticketing purposes.

Repeatedly high cancellation ratio.

Remarkable amounts of unticketed and cancelled bookings made within 24 hours of flight departure.


**2.2 Passive Bookings**

An ADM is issued /**25.-EUR **flat fee per PNR to the agency for all booking practices listed below will be collected.

Passive segments other than the passive bookings generated through the airline system ticketed by the agency.

Passive segments must not be used to create a copy when passenger requests an invoice or itinerary.

Passive bookings created by non-IATA agencies without ticketing authority.

All NO status coded segments that mean the reservation was refused by the airline.

Passive bookings created by the Travel Service Provider are rejected with the message “NO”.


All NO status codes must be deleted from PNR within the same day after this booking is rejected by the airline

**2.3 Duplicate Bookings**

An ADM is issued /**25.-EUR** flat fee will be collected as per the violation of duplicate bookings.

Reservations that are created for the same passenger by the same agency within one or more GDS system, are considered duplicate bookings

The conditions of duplicate bookings may contain,

The same flight number on the same or different date.

Flight segments in a PNR in active or passive status in different GDS systems.

Different flight numbers for the same city pair for the same or for a different date.

Different destination point on the same or different date.

Same or nearby airport.


**2.4 Test Bookings**

Bookings created in a live mode for agency training/ business tracking purposes / obtaining customer visa are considered as test bookings. An ADM with **25.-EUR **flat fee will be collected.

**2.5 Inactive Bookings**

Agent should monitor the queues regularly and remove all inactive segments. All inactive segments must be removed 24 hours prior to departure time. The following bookings are considered inactive bookings. An ADM with 25.-EUR flat fee will be collected.

All unremoved inactive HX, UN, UC, NO, SC, TK, US, PN or WK status codes in a PNR.

All unremoved waitlisted segments which are no longer needed.


**2.6 System and Connecting Flight Usage Abuse**

For bookings involving system and/or connecting abuse as specified below, the agent who made the transaction/issued the ticket will be fined 150.-EUR per passenger in the PNR for domestic flights and 300.-EUR per passenger in the PNR for international flights. In addition, the responsible agent's functions of viewing, booking and ticketing AJet Airlines flights may be blocked. In the event that the agency creating and ticketing the PNR is different, the systems of both agencies systems may be closed.

The transactions described below will be considered as “system and connecting flight abuse”.

Manipulation of a reservation with connecting flights.

Partial cancellation outside the rules.

Booking fake/unnecessary destinations in order to get a seat in a lower class.

Sending false information about the starting point of the journey.

Ticket issuance by adding fictitious destinations in order to reduce the applicable fare.

Keeping the transactions over a certain period of time without recording the PNR transaction.


**2.7 High ratio of ‘No-Show’ Bookings**

For such abuses, responsible agency will be subject to an ADM that is calculated as the highest fare on the related route. Any kind of ticketing causing remarkable no-shows, such as but not limited to;

No-shows occured for the segments created in order to be used just for ticketing the whole itinerary with a lower fare.

Remarkable amount of no-shows occured within a certain or different flights, etc.


**2.8 Uncommitted Bookings**

Uncommitted Bookings are seat sell attempts which are not finalized with EOT (End of Transaction). If they are kept longer than the usual time. Seats are blocked in airline flight inventory and disallow AJet to sell these seats before flight departure. Holding or blocking a seat on flight inventory is considered as an abuse and will be penalized with ADM of 50.-EUR flat fee in international flights and 20.-EUR flat in domestic flights.

**2.9 Group Reservations/ Hidden Groups**

Booking 10 or more passengers with at least one common flight and/or destination within their itinerary shall be requested as a group booking. Booking them under two or more individual PNRs is considered as “Hidden Group". Such bookings are subject to ADM of 50.-EUR flat fee in international flights.

**2.10 Churning**

Irrespective of the PNR, all booking and cancellation transactions created for the same passenger, same flight/s, same flight date/s for at least 5 times or more will be called as Churned Booking Transaction and will be subjected to ADM. The ADM amount will be 10.-EUR per segment.

**2.11 Contact Information of Passenger**

During the reservation process, the passenger's contact information (e-mail or phone number) must be entered correctly in the relevant field, In case the airline needs to contact the passenger, and the passenger cannot be reached due to incorrect contact details, the responsibility rests with the agency.

**3. Ticketing Procedures**

**TIME LIMIT AND TICKETING RULES**

Avoid “Churning”, excessive and repeated booking and cancelling of segments to bypass time limits or to meet GDS productivity

Avoid “No-Shows”; inventory abuse caused by agents failure to issue ticket and/or cancel unticketed reservations.

Avoid questionable cancellations prior to the scheduled flight.

Time limit requirements and fare rules must be taken into consideration. Ticket must be issued according to the booking status. Confirmed status may not be used unless received from VF

Avoid issuing confirmed tickets in unconfirmed bookings.

Agent must not create a PNR in order to check the fare and other information.

Reservation and ticketing classes of service should be the same.

Ticketing must be done in the same GDS in which the original booking is made.


**3.1.Contact**

You can send e-mail to [ADMDISPUTE@AJET .COM](mailto:ADMDISPUTE@AJET.COM) for your ADM related enquiries.

**3.2.Time Limit to Issue ADM**

For IATA and ARC agencies, time limits determined by these organisations will be applied. AJet issues ADMs within 9 months after the last date of travel for sales transactions/the refund date for refunded tickets.

For non-IATA agencies, ADMs will be issued within 1 year after the last date of travel/refund date


Time limits in ADM issuance do not infringe upon AJet right to issue/collect ADMs without being subject to any time limits in case of fraud and fraudulent transactions unless there are other restrictions (legal, administrative, commercial) as those time limits have been set to accomplish the audit.

**3.3. ADM Disputes**

ADMs will be communicated via BSPLINK for BSP agencies and Memo Manager for ARC agencies and via e-mail for non-IATA agencies.

Mind the following terms in case of ADM disputes:

Disputes should be raised within 14 days once the ADM is communicated with you. Otherwise, ADMs will be finalized and collection processes will be started immediately.

It is necessary to have supporting documents & information for disputes. Details should be stated.


**3.4 Minimum ADM Fee**

The audit department is obliged to issue an ADM for abuses which is more than 100 TRY for domestic departures and 10 EUR for international departures.

**3.5 Service Charge**

Service charge is 10 EUR for international departures and 50 TRY for domestic departures per each and every document.

The following terms will be applied as service charge:

Service charge will not be applied for minimum ADMs. However, for repeated transactions that do not breach the minimum ADM amount, a service fee of 50% of the repeated ADM amount will be applied.

Service charge will be added on the discrepancy and both the service charge and the ADM will be cancelled in case the ADM is invalid or null

Regarding commissions and taxes, minimum ADM amount and service charge don’t apply.

All ADM and ACM requests should be raised via BSP link. If the sales offices request an ADM, then there will be no service charge.

Service charge of 10 EUR will be also applied for ACMs for which AJet is not operationally responsible. In this case, service charge will be deducted from the ACM amount. Service charge for ACM is 20% of the ACM amount and cannot be more than 15 EUR.

There will be no service charge for ACMs in case of technical and systematic issues. There will also be no service charge ACMs issued in order to reverse an ADM.

Service charge equal to the amount of the bank commission will be applied to chargeback ADMs.


**3.6 Transactions Based on Documents**

Travel agencies are responsible to keep the documents for 1 year and to present the documents within 20 days once the audit department requests to audit them in case of tickets that require a document such as death, health report or denied boarding, etc.

Business partners/agencies are also responsible to keep a copy of the documents that provide a basis for the discount for 5 months and to present them within 20 days for discounted tickets if the audit department asks.

Failure to present the documents or to present invalid documents will eventually lead to ADMs that contains the difference between the discounted fare and the regular fare.

**3.7 Fraud and Fraudulent Transactions**

If it is determined that fraud and abuse transactions originate from the agency / agency business partner, the penalty for the benefit obtained through fraud is applied, taking into agreement the relevant contract provisions and amending them accordingly if necessary.

In case of abuse regarding ticketing transactions, penalties for improper transactions will be applied as described below:

In document-based transactions, if documents appropriate to the kind of the transaction cannot be presented or if the presented documents are fraudulent / invalid, a penalty of 25% of the ADM amount will be applied.

In cases where the agent manipulates the reservation and ticketing systems and causes financial damage (changing ticket fares and taxes, holding a seat with a fake ticket number, obtaining a lower class place by voiding the ticket 2 or more times, etc.), a 50 EUR penalty for international flights and 10 EUR for domestic flights will be applied.

If the baggage allowance for a package in the reservation system is changed during the ticketing phase, the amount misused will be calculated based on the fee charged per kilogram at the airport. A penalty of 10 EUR transaction fee will be added to this amount.

For incorrect transactions regarding Mode of payment, a penalty of 50 EUR will be issued for international flights and 10 EUR for domestic flights.

In case of use of discounted fares outside the scope, a penalty of 2 times the difference between the discounted fare used for each ticket and the system fee in the same fare class will be applied.


**3.8 Ticket Validity**

Unless otherwise specifically provided in the fare note, the validity for totally unused tickets is one year starting from the issue date of the original ticket and for the partly used tickets it is 1 year from the first date of travel. Ticket can be used depending on the fare validity (maximum stay) once travel starts.

Unless otherwise stated in the applicable fare notes; for the unused ticket, the validity, in case of re-issuing new ticket, is 1 year starting from the first flight day. If new ticket is not used and it is issue as an open ticket, the validity of the new ticket shall apply starting from the new issue date and it is 1 year again.

In case of re-issuing the new ticket once again (i.e. re-issuing the same ticket for 2 times over and over) the validity of the new ticket is again 1 year starting from the issue date if it is an open ticket and again 1 year starting from the date of first flight if it is not an open ticket.

Example

Totally unused ticket issued on 08JUN24

presented for reissue on 06JUN25 for a travel commencing on 07JUN25

Such ticket will have a validity till 07JUN26

**3.9 Rules for Rebooking and Rerouting**

In exchange transactions, the change penalty is charged as CP tax.

The remaining coupons should be open for exchange transactions

If the fare notes allow changes without penalty; if any penalty, collect fare and tax difference.

If the fare notes do not allow changes without penalty, then the penalty should not be collected only for the domestic flight. If penalty is applicable, fare and tax difference should also be collected.

If the fare notes do not allow any change, the ticket should not be changed.


Note that if there is “no-show” in the domestic part of an international ticket, then all “no-show” restrictions have to be applied under all circumstances.

Unless otherwise stated in the fare notes, exchange penalty for an adult is exactly the same for a child. In case of exchange, the penalty shall not be collected for an infant passenger if the infant does not reserve a seat.

**3.10 Rules for Exchange Applications**

In exchange transactions, the change penalty is charged as CP tax. The remaining coupons should be open for exchange transactions.

Exchange is converting a totally unflown ticket to a new ticket as a result of the passenger’s request for a change of flight number, date and route. Exchange ticket is issued based on applicable IROE and fares of new flight on the date of the transaction.

When an unused ticket is changed; if the fare note says 'New fare may be equal or higher', even if the fare for the new international travel is lower, the exchange fee cannot be reduced and the passenger cannot be refunded, therefore the new fare will be processed with equal or higher fares. If the existing ticket is refunded in accordance with the rule and a new ticket is issued in the passenger's interest, the passenger can be directed to the refund process.

While exchanging a ticket, even though the local fares of new ticket and original ticket are the same, there can be exchange rate differences. In such cases, if the exchange rate is higher, the difference should be collected.

**3.11 Rules for Reissue Applications**

In reissue transactions, the change penalty is charged as CP tax. The remaining coupons should be open for reissue transactions.

After the commencement of the travel, the passenger may request flight, date, route, or a class change. In this case, the original ticket should be reissued to a new ticket based on the fares valid on the original ticket’s issue date. Irrespective of currency used,

The ticket can be reissued outside the original country of issuance after the commencement of the travel, but reissue must be done by the pricing commands including original ticket date and original ticket city code.

While reissuing, there may be rate differences in local currency and the currency of the country of the reissue may also be different from the local currency as well. In this case, applicable exchange rates (FZS) of the transaction date should be used in order to convert local currency to the currency of the country of the reissue.

Where any fare is combined with another fare and only one fare component is changed, the penalty conditions of the changed fare component will apply for “reissue & no-show”. When more than one fare component is being changed, the highest penalty (more restricted) of all changed fare components for “reissue & no-show” will apply.

**REFUND RULES**

**3.12 Refund Validity and Refund Rules**

AJet tickets are valid for refunds up to 2 years from the date of issue of the original ticket. Ticket will lose validity for refund after 2 years from the date of issue of the original ticket although the last issued ticket may still be valid for travel because of echange/reissue transactions.

All fare notes of reissued/exchanged tickets should be checked retrospectively and refund restrictions should be studied carefully in case of refund in 2 years after the issue date of the original ticket.

If domestic sector of an international ticket has been used and if the passenger requests a refund, then recalculation should be done based on “after departure” fare notes even if the international sector of the ticket has not been flown and the calculation of the flown domestic sector should be done in EUR of domestic fares.

If the ticket is subject to “no-show” for the domestic sector of an international ticket, all “no-show” restrictions on fare notes should be applied in case of reissue/refund.


If a ticket (totally unused/partially used) is not refundable in terms of fare notes, unused YR and YQ tax will not be refunded as well. Unless otherwise stated, all unused taxes shall be refunded.

It is possible to end up with a negative value in case of refund for a refundable ticket (i.e. there may not be any fare for refund, on the contrary it is possible to encounter negative values to refund in local fares). It is not allowed to deduct this negative value from the refundable taxes. Moreover, all refundable taxes should be refunded even if there is no refundable amount for the original fare.

If the rule regarding the sequential use of ticket coupons in the fare notes is violated by any of the agency, call center, sales, or check-in personnel upon passenger request, if there is a refund for unused coupons, the fee for the used coupons and the taxes used will be calculated and the remaining amount will be refunded to the passenger.


**If there is a refund penalty in the fare notes;**

If the currency of the penalty is the same as the local fare of the ticket, it will be deducted from the local fare.

If the penalty is in the same currency as the local fare of the ticket but converted to a different currency in the original ticket, the penalty will be converted to the relevant currency at the exchange rate on the date the ticket was issued.

If the currency of the penalty is different from the local fare of the ticket and the equivalent currency, the penalty will be directly converted to the equivalent currency at the exchange rate on the date the ticket was issued.

According to the IATA rules, the refund of a ticket sold with a credit card is made to the name of the person in whose name the relevant credit card is.


**3.13 Coupon Status**

Before refunding electronic ticket, it is important to check the electronic ticket coupon status that is going to be refunded.

If there is any coupon status “flown” or “checked-in” is considered as open in addition to remaining open segments and refund amount is manipulated, ADM will be issued for this transaction.

**3.14 Refund of YR and YQ Taxes**

Under the paragraph of “Cancellations” in the fare notes;

YR and YQ taxes will not be paid back to the passenger in case of no-show for non-refundable tickets. This procedure should be considered in refund transactions.

**3.15 No Show Penalty**

If a passenger has a reserved seat but does not show up before departure, she/he is considered as “no-show “. It is possible to determine whether the passenger is subject to “no-show” or not by checking the departure time of the ticket even if the passenger has either a new reservation or a new flight on his PNR record. This “no-show” definition is not applicable for tickets that only have domestic flights.

On the other hand, in accordance with the rule “CHANGE IS BEFORE ORIGINAL SCHEDULED FLIGHT” stated in the fare notes, passenger’s request of change must be reflected on the ticket before the original flight’s scheduled departure. Otherwise the passenger will be accepted as “no-show” and should be acted in accordance with the no-show rules in CAT16.

In sum, in order to decide whether a ticket is subject to “no-show” or not, the time of the transaction on the ticket should be taken into consideration. The time of the flight cancellation does not have an impact..

**3.16 Fare/ Class Differences**

Class, represents groups for diversified fares depending on different factors such as minimum/maximum stay rule, advance purchase rule etc. although the cabin and the seats are the same.

In some cases the class booked (reserved) and the class of the ticket issued are different and it is seen that some agencies issue tickets with the fare of the class booked (reserved). However, this is an incorrect practice. Ticket must be issued with the fare of the ticket issued. In case there is a difference between the class booked and the class of the ticket issued, an ADM will be issued if there is an uncollected fare difference between the original and the final ticket.

**3.17 Payment Methods**

AJet has the right to determine the type of card used as a payment method within the scope of IATA TIP (Resolution 890) Regulations. Our agencies are required to obtain the written approval of the AJet sales offices regarding the card types they will use. In the event of the usage of an unapproved card type, AJet reserves the right to issue an ADM at a rate of 2% of the total amount of each ticket, with a minimum of **25 EUR** per segment.
