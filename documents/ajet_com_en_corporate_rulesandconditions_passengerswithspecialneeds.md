## Passengers with Special Needs

In order to complete their transactions in time, we advise our passengers to be at the airport at least 1 hour before the domestic flight and 2 hours before the international flight.

### Pregnant Passengers

Dear pregnant passengers, please note the following rules to spend a more comfortable and safe journey.

-
Any report for travel will not be asked from our pregnant passengers who have not completed their twenty-eight weeks (seven months) of pregnancy.

-
Our passengers who are pregnant with a single baby, can travel from the beginning of the 28th week to the end of the 35th week as long as they receive a report from their doctors stating "there is not any risk of traveling by plane". In and after thirty-six weeks, pregnant passengers are not allowed to travel, even if they receive a report from the doctor.

-
Our passengers who are pregnant with two or more babies can travel with a report that they receive from their doctors stating “there is not any risk of traveling by plane “from the beginning of the 28th week until the end of the 31st week. In and after thirty-two weeks, they are not allowed to travel even though they have a report from their doctors.

-
Doctor's report date cannot be older than seven days.

-
The report must be written by a specialist doctor related to the subject matter, on letterhead paper belonging to the doctor or the healthcare institution they work for; it must include the doctor’s name, surname, diploma number, signature and the date of issuance of the report.

-
The language of the report must be in Turkish or English.


### Passengers who require Medical Permission

A medical doctor's report is required for our passengers, who require medical permission, to travel safely. The conditions required for different situations are as follows.

-
**Our passengers who have undergone operation:**passengers who have undergone any kind of heart, vascular, chest and brain diseases or related operations (by-pass, angioplasty, heart surgery, brain surgery, etc.) within the last 3 months can fly with a medical doctor's report. -
**Our passengers traveling with medical assistance:**passengers with asthma and other chronic lung diseases, respiratory failure due to heart disease, and passengers who will travel with medical assistance such as catheters, resistors, etc. can fly with a medical doctor's report. -
**Our infant passengers:**infant passengers older than two days and younger than seven days can fly with a medical doctor's report. -
**Our pregnant passengers:**passengers who are 28-35 weeks pregnant with one baby (beginning of 28th week, end of 35th week) and passengers who are 28-31 weeks pregnant with two or more babies (beginning of 28th week, end of 31st week) can fly with a medical doctor’s report. -
**Our passengers with cancer:**passengers with cancer, whose therapy and treatment is ongoing, can fly with a medical doctor's report. -
**Our passengers with MS:**in case of MS (Multiple Sclerosis) disease, passengers with the possibility of sudden development of attacks can fly with a medical doctor’s report. (MS is not a mental illness, but it does have acute periods that affect cognitive behavior.) -
**Our passengers with Parkinson's disease:**they can fly with a medical doctor's report. There should be a statement in the report that they can travel with or without accompaniment. -
**Our passengers with Alzheimer's and dementia:**they can travel with a medical doctor's report and only with an accompanying person. -
**Our passengers with epilepsy:**they can fly with a medical doctor's report. If the passenger having an epileptic seizure or suffering from syncope (fainting) before the flight declares that they are fine afterwards and wants to fly, it is requested that the doctor’s report be updated. -
**Our passengers with SMA:**they can fly with a medical doctor's report. -
**Our unaccompanied passengers with autism:**they are admitted to the flight with a medical doctor's report stating “They can travel without a companion”. A medical doctor's report shall not be demanded from individuals with autism if they are traveling with an accompanying person on AJet flights.

**CAUTION: **
__The medical doctor's report bearing the expression "The passenger is fit to travel by air." shall be requested in respect of any diseases declared by the passengers, other than the above mentioned situations.__

-
**If our passengers with food allergies***do not have their report with themselves, they are not accepted to the flight, as the aircraft has not been purified in advance; and if the passenger demands a ticket change, the normal ticket rules are applied.

However, if the passenger withdraws his/her statement and requests to join the flight, then the passenger shall be notified that the entire responsibility of the health problems that the passenger may experience in this regard shall belong to him/her, and the passenger shall be admitted to the flight on the condition that all responsibility belongs to the passenger.

* Passengers who declare their food allergies (nuts, peanuts, etc.) at the airport before the flight.

**CAUTION: **
__The full responsibility for the potential health problems any passenger who is allergic to certain foods (nuts, peanuts, etc.) might have during the flight belongs to the passenger.__

-
***Passengers requesting medical oxygen cylinder:**they can fly with a medical doctor's report.

-
If the passenger's medical status is suspected in any way (cough, vomiting, rash on the body, bleeding, etc.), AJet has the right to request a report from the passenger.


**Medical Doctor’s Report Rules**

-
Medical doctor’s report shall bear the expression “The passenger is fit to travel by air” absolutely.

-
Medical doctor's report shall have been obtained within the last 10 days.

-
Such report shall have been written on the letterhead of the medical doctor or health-care organization. It must be issued in Turkish or English.

-
Date of issue of the report shall be necessarily specified. The full name, diploma number and signature of the medical doctor, who has issued the report, shall be necessarily specified on the report.

-
In the event that it is specified on the medical doctor's report, the passengers who require medical permission travel as accompanied. Accompaniment service shall not be provided by the cabin crews for the passengers who require medical permission.


### Visually and Hearing Impaired Passengers

-
Visually and hearing impaired passengers are not asked a medical report, they can travel unaccompanied. However, both visually and hearing impaired passengers cannot travel unaccompanied.

-
Guide dogs are accepted in the cabin free of charge by making a reservation. Assisting dogs accompanying the passengers are taken without cage to the cabin. The dog should be clean, his mouth should be tied, and should sit just near the feet of his owner. The guide animal to be admitted to the aircraft must have a valid medical document, an identity document, a vaccination sheet, or documents required by the country of destination, and they must be whole and complete.


### Passengers with Limited Mobility

-
Reports from disabled passengers are not required, but the disabled passengers who cannot meet their own needs and evacuate from the aircraft on their own if necessary are not allowed to travel without their companion.


**Note:** The cabin crew unfortunately cannot help you with breathing apparatus, food, medicine tracking, and toilet needs during flight. But they can help you on the way from and to the door of the toilet for toilet needs.

### Who is not accepted?

-
Passengers who are obliged to use electrical or pneumatic tools during flight (except for oxygen cylinder and portable breathing apparatus provided by AJet)

-
Passengers suffering an asthma attack before closing the aircraft door,

-
The new-born babies within their first 48 hours,

-
Passengers with infectious disease are not admitted to the flight at all during the contagious period.


### Private Health Service

Transfers between the terminal and the aircraft are carried out professionally for our passengers with wheelchair.

This service is provided by qualified personnel of the health and / or ground service organizations where AJet is assigned for 24 hours.

In addition, the wheelchair team immediately evaluates your requests at the check-in counter.

To benefit from the accompanying services, please do not forget to mention your request at least 24 hours during your reservation.

**Private health service is provided in all the cities where AJet flies.**

You should book at least 48 hours before your flight to ensure that the following additional services are available in a best possible way.

If you give less than 48 hours’ notice, the airport may not be able to provide the service as promptly as you need.

To ensure your journey goes smoothly, making reservation for you need is really important.

### Wheelchair

Depending on the passenger’s condition, 3 different types of wheelchair service are provided to passengers who do not have a medical disorder but need a wheelchair until 24 hours is left to your first flights (internal and external) on our website.

-
For Apron: For the passengers who can go up and down the stairs but cannot walk the long way, it is a wheelchair provided up to the plane. The passenger requests the wheelchair for transport from the plane to the passenger lounge or from the passenger lounge to the plane. There is no problem with climbing up and descending the stairs. S/he can walk to the seats in the cabin.

-
For the steps: For the passengers who cannot go up and down the stairs but are able to walk, the wheelchair is provided until the aircraft gate. The passenger requests the wheelchair for transport from the plane to the passenger lounge or from the passenger lounge to the plane. There is a problem with climbing up and descending the stairs. S/he can walk to the seats in the cabin.

-
Till Cabin: For passengers who cannot walk, it is a wheelchair supplied up to the seat in the cabin. Passenger is completely immobile and cannot move at all. S/he needs help for steps of the aircraft. It is compulsory that they are transported from the aircraft door to the cabin seat, and from the cabin seat to the aircraft door.


If you do not make a request during the booking and sales phase, the desired service may be delayed at the airport.

### Important Note regarding Wheel Chair

-
As well as our passengers with special request demands, online check-in, Kiosk Check-in and Online Boarding Card services cannot be provided for our wheelchair request passengers.

-
If the passenger declares that he can fly unaccompanied, no companion condition is required.

-
Assuming the disabled passenger travels on the same aircraft, wheelchair or necessary equipment, medical supplies etc. are transported free of charge in the Cargo compartment.

-
The responsibility for disconnecting the battery connection/energy of wheelchairs operating on dry, liquid, and lithium batteries/accumulators lies with the passenger and/or their companion during acceptance.

-
In addition, wheelchair requests cannot be fulfilled for travels with flights operated jointly with other airlines in the travel plan.

-
We do not have wheelchair service for our baby passengers.

-
The battery of a wheelchair or other mobility device must not exceed 300 Wh. For devices equipped with two batteries, each battery must not exceed 160 Wh. Wheelchairs or mobility devices with removable batteries may be transported as checked baggage without restrictions, provided they meet these requirements.

-
Passengers may carry one spare lithium-ion battery not exceeding 300 Wh, or two spare batteries not exceeding 160 Wh each.

-
Batteries removed from wheelchairs or other mobility devices, as well as spare batteries, must be carried in the cabin.

-
Mobility devices with lithium-ion batteries that do not meet these conditions are not accepted for carriage.
