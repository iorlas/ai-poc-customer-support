**CIP Lounge Terms of Use**

Miles&Smiles Elite, Elite Plus or Elite Corporate members who travel with AJet domestic flights can enjoy this experience without entry fee.

Miles&Smiles Classic and Classic Plus members can access the domestic passenger lounges listed below at the prices specified by Turkish Airlines.

Passengers who are not Miles&Smiles members can have a digital card by filling out online membership form and can enjoy the lounges at the prices specified by Turkish Airlines.

The lounges included are at the following airports: Sabiha Gokcen, Antalya, İzmir Adnan Menderes, Milas-Bodrum, Dalaman, Ankara Esenboga, Cukurova, Kayseri, Gaziantep, Hatay, Trabzon, Rize-Artvin, and Diyarbakır.

CIP Lounge online sales can only be made for Sabiha Gökçen International Airport Lounge on AJet.com, AJet Mobile, Call Center and Agency
