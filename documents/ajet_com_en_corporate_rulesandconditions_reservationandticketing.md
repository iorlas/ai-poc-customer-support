## Reservation And Ticketing

**General Rules for Reservation and Ticketing**

**Ticket Fee and Selected Route:**Ticket fees are applicable in the country where the journey begins. When the ticket is purchased from another country, the ticket fee may vary depending on the rules and season of the fee. For domestic flights within Turkey, the fees displayed in Turkish Lira apply if you purchase your ticket through our website.**Maximum Number of Passengers and Flights:**When making reservations through our website, you can book for a maximum of 9 passengers and up to six flights at one time.**Ticket Change:**You can make changes through our website, mobile application, agencies, or call center. After entering your PNR and surname in the Flight Management section, the process is carried out by obtaining an activation code.**Free Ticket Change and Cancellation Within 24 Hours:**Tickets purchased through the AJet website and AJet Mobile application can be changed or canceled once, free of charge, within 24 hours of purchase in accordance with the change and cancellation policies. The process can be completed by entering the reservation code (PNR) and surname under the Flight Management section and then receiving an activation code. For more detailed information, please visit the[Fare Notes](https://ajet.com/en/corporate/rules-and-conditions/fare-notes)page.AJet reserves the right to change the aircraft type and seat selections.

The process of keeping tickets open is not available for Domestic and International Flights.


**Ticketing**

For flights eligible for electronic ticketing, an electronic ticket will be issued if you pay the ticket fee via our website with a credit card or alternative payment methods for reservations made between 355 days and a minimum of 2 hours before the first flight.

It is not mandatory for the person whose credit card is used for payment to be among the passengers in the reservation record. If a passenger approaches the counter for any change/cancellation/refund request or for check-in procedures for the first flight in the reservation record, the owner of the credit card used for payment must also be present with a valid ID to present the credit card used for payment. If the credit card used for payment is a virtual card, please also bring the credit card linked to the virtual card with you.

For self check-in, please use the credit card you used for payment. If the credit card holder is not among the traveling passengers and/or if the credit card used for payment is a virtual card, you cannot use our self check-in service. You can view your e-ticket document, which includes your electronic ticket information and acts as an invoice, by clicking on the link [ebiletfatura.ajet.com](http://ebiletfatura.ajet.com)

**Refunds and Cancellations:U/W/P/V Classes;**

In cancellation/refund transactions, only the collected Airport Ground Taxes are refunded.

For cancellation/amendment transactions, the activation code is obtained after entering the PNR and surname from the Flight Management tab.

In the cancellation transactions made through our website, an information e-mail is sent to the passenger by AJet.

Tickets are not refunded after the flight is operated and tickets with a non-refundable rule are non-refundable. On these flights, you can only get a refund of the refundable taxes charged when the ticket was issued.

The refund of the ticket is made in accordance with the payment method used when purchasing the ticket.

Tickets paid by credit card, debit card, BKM Express, iDEAL and Sofort can be refunded through our website.

The refund of tickets paid with iDEAL and Sofort will be transferred to your account within approximately 5 business days.

If the credit card you used for payment has expired or your card has been canceled, your refund will be made to your card if you use another card of the same bank. If you no longer work with this bank, you can apply to the call center with an official letter from the bank.

Refunds do not include the ticketing service fee and no service fee will be charged for the refund. Tickets issued through our call center or by agencies are not refunded through our online channels (website/mobile application). Please contact our call center for the refund of these tickets.

A confirmation e-mail will be sent to you for cancellation/refund transactions made through our online channels (website/mobile application).

**Ticketing Service Fee General Rules**

These levels and rules under the Ticketing Service Fee heading are only valid at points where Ticketing Service Fee is applicable.

Ticketing Service Fee is not refundable in case of ticket cancellation/refund.

Ticketing Service Fee is charged for all passengers except infants.

For connecting domestic tickets, the level of the long track is charged, and for international tickets, the level of the departure point is charged.

In the event that reservations created through online channels are ticketed outside of these channels, the 'Ticketing Service Fee' valid on the channel in question will be applied in the ticketing process. Fee difference may occur as a result of ticketing the reservations made through online channels at the call center.

In the event that the cabin changes during ticketing on the basis of track for international ticketing, the Ticketing Service Fee level will be charged according to the highest fare class.

AJet reserves the right to change the rules and fare levels regarding Ticketing Service Fee..


**Seat Selection**

Seat selection is valid on all AJet flights.

Seat selection can be made at the ticket purchase stage or during check-in.

Seat selection can be made through the website, mobile application, call center and agency channels.

Passengers with infants can only travel in window seats (except emergency exit row).

For group passengers (bookings with 10 or more passengers), the seat selection application can only be purchased through the call center and authorized agents.

Passengers who select seats up to 24 hours before the flight must check in before the flight.

The number of paid selectable seats on a flight may vary depending on aircraft type, peak demand and time.

**Emergency Exit Seat Selection Special Rules**

According to "Passenger Placement Circular for Emergency Exit Seats" of the Civil Aviation General Directorate;

In the event of an unexpected need for evacuation, the passenger traveling on the emergency exit seat is expected to assist in opening the emergency exit gate and helping to evacuate the aircraft. For this reason;

To be able to understand written or oral instructions in Turkish or English,

must be at least 18 years old,

must not have a physical obstacle that limits his mobility.

The passenger must be willing, as well as able to assist in the case of an emergency evacuation. Passengers sitting emergency exit seats should submit that provided conditions when requested.

The passengers in the following situations can not sit in the emergency exit row seat;

İnfant and child passengers even if it is under the supervision of his / her parents,

The passengers whose mobility is partially or completely restricted,

The passengers who demand that second seat belt,

The passengers and companions who are judicial proceedings and administrative cases.

AJet has full authority determine whether it provides the requirements of sitting emergency exit seats. Passengers who are not provide requirements will transfer an alternative seat. The additional amount paid for an emergency exit seat will not be refunded.

**Changes to Seat**The seat selected by the passenger for a fee may be changed even after boarding for safety, security and operational reasons. AJet has full discretion to decide whether the seat purchased by the passenger meets his/her seating requirements. AJet provides suitable alternative options in such cases. The passenger will not be refunded the seat selection amount.

The passenger who has made a seat selection can only use this right for himself/herself and cannot transfer it to another passenger.

If the passenger requests a change in date/time/route of their flight, their seat preference will be transferred to the new flight or the seat selection fee will be refunded.


**Cancel/Refunds**

If the passenger cancels their flight at their own request, they can request a refund of the paid seat selection fee until the flight hour.

Passengers who have purchased excess baggage or sports equipment can go to the sales offices for cancellation / refund, provided that they have never used this right.

If the flight is canceled/changed by the passenger without his/her request; There is a right to a refund request for the additional services selection fee.

On flights where the passenger is not satisfied due to a seat change other than his/her own request; There is a right to a refund request for the seat selection fee. If this happens in the cabin, it shall be requested that the case be taken under the cabin team.

The period of use for reserved tickets is 12 months starting from the first flight date on the ticket and the refund period is 24 months starting from the first flight date. However, since the usage and refund periods of tickets issued with some special fares available on our flights may differ from the above-mentioned periods according to the fare rules, you can contact the sales office,

[Call Center](https://ajet.com/en/contact)or your agency for more detailed information.

**Overdelivery**

Although airline passengers have tickets and reservations, passengers may not come to their flights for various reasons. In order to reduce the effects caused by these passengers (no show) and to provide all the passengers with the desired flight, AJet sometimes sell these overbooks like all the world airlines. Even thought transporters take precautions to avoid an unintended situation, there is a risk that some passengers cannot be flown even though their flights are reserved and confirmed. When such a situation is encountered, AJet provides compensation and additional services to those passengers in accordance with the solidarity system directive. You can also get detailed information from our offices and check-in counters.

**Double Booking**

All reservations made from our website and other sales channels are automatically controlled by our system. Only one of your reservations that have been determined to be repeated will be valid according to the booking rules and all the other records will be canceled without notice. Please be careful not to make double reservation.

Pursuant to the agreement between the Turkish Republic and the Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus on “the Traveling of the Turkish Citizens to the Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus and the Traveling of the Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus Citizens to the Turkish Republic with an Identification Document instead of a Passport”, minors and interdicted persons who have come to the border crossings with their identity to enter the TRNC need to submit their notorized and officially confirmed letter of consent which indicates the permission of their legal representatives for the exiting procedure.
