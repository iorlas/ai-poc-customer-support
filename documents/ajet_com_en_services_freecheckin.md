### Warnings

We recommend that you carry your processes out from our Self Check-in kiosks to get your boarding passes.

Check-in process begins 24 hours prior to your flight. You can save time by completing online check-in 24 hours before your flight.

Check-in facilities at airports close

__45 minutes__before take-off for domestic flights and__60 minutes__before take-off for international flights. This rule may vary according to the customs of local authorities at some stations.You must have received your boarding passes at least 45 minutes before domestic flights and 60 minutes international flights departure time and have completed your luggage handling processes.

In cases where you need to get your ticket at the airport or if your ticket needs to be processed, you need to apply for the sales bank one hour before your departure time.

You may have exceeded your luggage allowance. Please plan your arrival at the airport, taking the length of your excess luggage payment into consideration.

AJet has the right to change the seat number you have selected at check-in, in accordance with operational requirements.


### Cancellation of Check-In

You can make your check-in cancellation by calling our [Call Center.](https://ajet.com/en/contact)

### Self Check-in

With Self Check-in, our passengers can;

Perform their check-in process for both themselves and other passengers under the same reservation record and can choose a paid seat, get a luggage tag, make payments in case of excess baggage fees.

Also carry out a "Return Check-in" if they have return flight on the same day, provided that they don't have any luggage.

If passengers have no luggage, they go directly to the flight gate with the boarding passes to be printed after their processes are complete.

If passengers have luggage, they deliver their luggage at the "Luggage Delivery Reception" after receiving the boarding passes, and then go to the flight gate.


### Self Check-in used Stations:

İstanbul Sabiha Gökçen, Ankara, Antalya, İzmir and Ercan.

### Self Check-in

Self Check-in devices are designed to be used by touching the simple menu screen that will display on the screen.

In order to access the credentials, the Electronic Ticket Numbers or Reservation codes/PNRs must be entered, Self check-in starts at this point and check-in process is completed by processing the required menus.
