## Seat Selection

### General Rules

The seat selection is applicable for all AJet flights.

Seat selection can be made through the AJet website and mobile application, call center and agency channels.

The seat can be selected while or after booking the ticket; it cannot be made for the optional reservations.

The passengers who have selected their seats are required to complete their check-in transactions before the flight.

The passengers with infants can travel in the window seats only (except for the emergency exit row).

Passengers with illness and/or reduced mobility can select aisle or window seats (in such a manner as not to block the cabin emergency exit).

Extra-legroom seats and emergency exit seats are not suitable for passengers traveling with a pet, a guide dog or an emotional support dog, who have purchased an extra seat and who have purchased extra seats to carry special equipment such as musical instruments in the cabin.

The seat selection can be booked for the group passengers (reservations that include 10 or more passengers) through the call centers and authorized agents only.

The number of the seats that can be selected for a fee may vary by aircraft type, size of demand, and time.

### Emergency Exit Row Seat Selection Special Rules

As per the “Circular on Passenger Seating on Emergency Exit Seats” issued by the Directorate General of Civil Aviation,

In the event that evacuation is needed due to a contingency, the passenger traveling in the emergency exit row seat is expected to help opening the emergency exit door and evacuation of the aircraft. For this reason, the passenger must

Be able to understand Turkish or English written and verbal instructions,

16 years of age or older,

Have no physical disability restricting their mobility.


The passengers seated in the emergency exit row seats must confirm upon request that they meet the necessary conditions and are willing to help the evacuation.

The following passengers may not be seated in the emergency exit row seats:,

Infant and child passengers, even if they are supervised by their parents,

Passengers with partially or totally restricted mobility,

Passengers who request the second seat belt,

The passengers who are subjected to judicial proceedings and administrative actions and their escorts.


AJet is fully authorized to decide on whether the passenger meets the requirements of being seated in the emergency exit row seat. The passengers who do not meet the requirements are assigned to the alternative seats.


### Extra Seat

An extra seat for domestic and international flights may be bought for only one of the adult or child passengers specified in the PNR. For the passengers who demand extra seat, a single ticket is issued; and the total payment is inclusive of the base fare and fuel expense.

The extra seat to be selected can only be adjacent vacant seat in the same row with the passenger selecting the seat. The seats in the immediate front or back row or the aisle side seats cannot be regarded as side by side. The extra seat selection cannot be used for the other passengers in the reservation.

The passenger who has extra seat can make seat selection free of charge (except for the emergency exit and legroom seats).

The extra seat selection can be made while booking a ticket only. It cannot be made after ticketing or during the check-in.

For the passenger for whom extra seat has been selected, it is mandatory and free of charge to select a seat during booking. For other passengers in the ticket, it is possible to make seat selection free of charge or for a fee during booking, as per the package rules.

For the passenger who has extra seat, it is not possible to change the seat selection on online channels during check-in. During online check-in process, our passengers can continue their transactions with the extra seat they selected while booking.

Extra seat can be sold for adult and child passengers on online channels. Extra seat cannot be sold for the tickets that include any one of the other passenger types.

Even if no seat has been selected for the other passengers in the reservation, it is possible to make transaction through the online channels as per the booking and check-in rules.

The passengers who have made extra seat selection may change their seats only at the counters in the airport.

The extra seats can be bought until the ticketing period ends.

Passenger who has extra seat can create a boarding pass only for the seat s/he has chosen. No different boarding pass can be created for extra seat.

In case of change, refund, or cancellation of the ticket of the passenger who has extra seat, the original ticket rules and conditions apply.

Of the passengers whose extra seat selecting processes have been completed on the online channels, the other transactions after the booking process (such as refund, change, adding new flight or infant passenger) are made through our call center, or the agents from which they have purchased the ticket.


### Change

The seat chosen by the passenger for his/her fee; It can be changed even after boarding the aircraft for safety, security and operational reasons. AJet has full authority to decide whether the passenger meets the seating requirements in the seat he has purchased. AJet provides suitable alternative options in such cases.

The passengers who have selected their seat can use this right for themselves only; they may not transfer this right to another passenger. The seat selection fee will not be refunded to the passenger.

If the passenger requests a change in date/time/route of their flight until the flight hour, their seat preference will be transferred to the new flight or the seat selection fee will be refunded.


### Cancellation/Refund

If the passenger cancels their flight willingly, the seat selection amount is not refunded.

If AJet changes any one of the flights, or cancels the flights, the selected seats may be canceled. In that case, a refund request for the seat fee can be made to our call center.

If the flight is canceled/changed against the passenger’s will, the passenger is entitled to demand refund of the seat selection amount.

If the passenger is assigned to an alternative seat for operational reasons after boarding, a refund request for the seat fee can be made to our call center.

The passenger is required to apply to the call center for refund demands within no later than 1 year from the flight date.

If the passenger replaces their seat with a cheaper or more expensive seat, the new fare is paid by the passenger, and the previous payment is not refunded.
