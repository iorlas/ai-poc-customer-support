## Baggage

### General Luggage Information

[Click here](https://ajet.com/pdfs/ajet-general-conditions-of-carriage.pdf)to see the General Conditions of Carriage.Baggage refers to the clothes and special items belonging to the passengers. There are two types of luggage, registered and unregistered (cabin baggage).

Your baggage will be transported to its final destination point in accordance with the customs regulations in the country of destination. Please consult your check-in staff to find out whether you can pick up your baggage at the final destination point.

Always check your baggage for hazardous substances that are not allowed to be transported.

Please prepare your own baggage or supervise its preparation for your safety. While preparing your baggage, be careful to check all the closed boxes, packages and packing materials. Please do not accept any packages that are given to you in order to pass on to another person.

Unacceptable items in registered or unregistered baggage may vary according to the countries. Please ask for information about the applications in the countries of arrival and departure while purchasing your airline ticket.

You can carry your precious items such as jewelry, money, mobile phones, computers, documents, etc. in your cabin baggage.

If you cannot find your baggage at the airport upon arrival or if you encounter any damage, you can find information about our offered services under our Baggage Delays page.

Passengers may not transport in their Checked Baggage any computers or other portable electronic devices, money, medicines, keys, jewelry, gold or silver items, precious metals, negotiable instruments, securities or other valuables, promissory notes or other commercial valuable papers, business documents, passports, other identification documents or samples, unplugged batteries or portable power supplies. The airlines will not be liable for any loss or damage to such items if they are placed in passenger's checked baggage despite this prohibition.


Which situations will cause your baggage not to be accepted?

Your baggage will not be accepted under the following conditions:

If it may cause damage to the aircraft, passengers or other cargo,

If it is not properly packaged for a smooth and safe transport,

If it contains items which are prohibited by law or regulations in any of the departing, arriving or transiting states,

If its dimensions exceed the size limits.

If the weight of one piece exceeds 32 kg,

If the baggage contains hazardous substances that are prohibited

### Cabin Baggage Rules

If the size and/or weight of personal items exceed the personal item limits, they are accepted as cabin baggage.

If bag-type baby strollers cannot be placed under the seats or in top-shelf cabinets and / or cannot be safely tied to the seat, they will be taken to the Cargo deck without charge.

Cabin baggage control is carried out during check-in and during the boarding.

If the cabin baggage exceeds the weight limit, the passenger is charged a baggage fee per piece. It is given under the plane to be carried in the cargo compartment.

If the cabin baggage complies with the weight limits but is outside the limits in terms of size, the passenger will be charged a baggage fee per piece. is given under the plane to be carried in the cargo compartment.

For our passengers who have completed the online / mobile / kiosk check-in process, Cabin baggage control is carried out during boarding. Our passengers are obliged to present their online / mobile / kiosk check-in documents.

If it is determined that our passenger has brought baggage other than the cabin baggage that he/she is entitled to carry, baggage fee per piece will be charged. It is given under the plane to be carried in the cargo compartment.

In the boarding process cabin luggage measurement unit for size checks, digital hand weighing for weight control and / or airport weighing are used. Cabin baggage that is labeled cabin baggage but is deemed unsuitable for weight and size is checked again.

The non-tagged cabin baggage size and weight control is carried out by the staff in charge. Luggage not fitted with "Cabin Baggage Labels" is considered to have not been checked.

Inspection of the dangerous goods which can not be transported in the cargo compartment but which can not be carried in the cabin and which can be carried in the cabin about the baggage to be transported in the cargo compartment is labeled. Unsuitable dangerous items transported in cargo compartments are not accepted as registered baggage. In addition, it is absolutely necessary to ask the traveler whether they have travel documents and medicines on board in the cabin.

### Carrying Liquids in Cabin Baggage

All the materials such as liquids, gels, aerosols etc. which are carried in the cabin baggage are subject to some restrictions in accordance with the aviation regulations

### Rules:

Liquids should be transported in the containers of up to 100 ml each.

These containers must be transported separately in transparent and lockable bags measuring 20x20 cm in size, not exceeding 1 liter.

Each passenger can carry only 1 bag.

At the last safety checkpoint, you will be asked to remove the locked bag from your bag and pass it through the x-ray device separately.


### Exceptions:

The following items can be carried in the cabin:

Medicines (Medicines must be in their original packaging, a prescription containing the names of the relevant medicines and the name and surname of the passenger, or a medical report indicating that “these medicines should be used “must also be submitted)

Baby foods (On the condition that the baby is traveling on the same plane and the amount of baby food must not exceed the required amount for the duration of the journey)

At the checkpoint, security officers may ask you to document your exceptional circumstance or to taste a medicine/ baby food / liquid.


### Restricted Liquids:

All kinds of liquids, including water, syrup, drinks,

Any make-up material including mascara (except lipsticks in solid form), and creams, lotions, oils (including cosmetic oils), cologne, perfumes,

All kinds of paste products, including shaving foam, deodorants, tooth paste,

All the non-solid Foods and jellies, such as jam, honey, yogurt, molasses and tomato paste,

Contact lens fluids, shampoos, sprays and aerosols, and other substances similar to those listed above.


### Liquid Restrictions in Hand Baggage:

Up to 1-liter volume,

Lockable and

Carried in transparent bags,

Medicine required during the flight (with prescription and original packaging),

Baby products are exempt from this practice.


### Transport of Liquid Items Purchased from Duty-Free Stores

Liquids from duty-free stores must be transported in closed and specials bags along with their invoices.

Please do not open the closed bags until the final destination point of your travel. Otherwise, the contents of the bag can be confiscated at the security checkpoints.

It is not allowed to carry alcoholic beverages in cabin baggage other than closed duty-free products and to consume them during travel.

Duty-free products (over 100 ml) you have purchased in advance, if you are in a connecting flight from a European country or USA, cannot be transported as hand luggage even in locked bags.

### Health Equipment Wheelchair:

Wheelchairs are transported free of charge, provided that the disabled passenger travels on the same plane.

The battery of a wheelchair or other mobility device must not exceed 300 Wh. For devices equipped with two batteries, each battery must not exceed 160 Wh. Wheelchairs or mobility devices with removable batteries may be transported as checked baggage without restrictions, provided they meet these requirements.

Passengers may carry one spare lithium-ion battery not exceeding 300 Wh, or two spare batteries not exceeding 160 Wh each.

Batteries removed from wheelchairs or other mobility devices, as well as spare batteries, must be carried in the cabin.

Mobility devices with lithium-ion batteries that do not meet these conditions are not accepted for carriage.

**Transporting Smart Baggage**

Smart baggage is the term used for baggage that consists of devices that include integrated lithium batteries that allow for the charging of external devices, and have extra capabilities such as GPS tracking devices, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, self-weighing capabilities and digital locks. Smart baggage whose lithium batteries cannot be detached are not allowed as cabin baggage or check-in baggage.

Smart baggage can be allowed as cabin and check-in baggage, if the following conditions are met.

**Transporting smart baggage in cabin**

The lithium battery has to be detached from the smart baggage, carried as cabin baggage and cannot be reinserted until the destination is reached.

To protect the detached lithium battery from short-circuiting, the battery should be placed in its original packaging, or its terminals should be insulated, or the battery should be placed in secure plastic bags.

**Transporting smart baggage as check-in baggage**

The lithium battery has to be detached from the smart baggage and carried separately in the cabin.

The above rules do not apply for smart baggage that is powered by lithium button cells.

__Special Circumstances__

The above rules are not applied for smart baggage powered by lithium button cells.

Portable vehicles that can operate with a lithium-ion battery such as hoverboards, segways, airwheels, solowheels, balance wheels, electric and battery operated bicycles, etc. are prohibited from being carried in the cabin or as check-in baggage on flights

The only element that can pose a danger in carrying drones used for hobby purposes in the cabin is the type and capacity of the battery they contain. There is no problem in carrying battery-containing drones in the cabin within the restrictions regarding electronic devices containing lithium batteries specified in the ICAO Doc.9284 Technical Instructions.

In cases where the batteries of wheelchairs / other mobility devices with liquid batteries need to be removed, the removed battery must be placed in a safe and hard package by the passenger. If the battery is not packaged properly, it will not be loaded onto the aircraft.
