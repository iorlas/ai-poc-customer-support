## Free Check In

**Warnings**

You can easily complete your check-in via

[AJet.com](http://AJet.com), AJet Mobile app, or our kiosks, saving time at the airport and starting your journey without delays.Airport check-in closes 45 minutes before domestic flights and 60 minutes before international flights. These times may vary depending on local regulations.

For domestic flights, you must have your boarding pass and completed baggage check at least 45 minutes before departure, and for international flights, at least 60 minutes before.

If you need to obtain your ticket at the airport or have any adjustments made to your ticket, you must visit the check-in counter at least one hour before your flight.

You may exceed your baggage allowance. Please plan your arrival at the airport accordingly to handle excess baggage.

AJet reserves the right to change the seat you have selected during check-in, depending on operational requirements.


**Cancellation of Check-in**

If you wish to cancel your check-in, please contact our Call Center for assistance.**1. Online Check-in**

Through online check-in, you can check in for both yourself and other passengers under the same booking, and also select your seats.

For connecting or round-trip flights, you can check in for both flights at the same time when there is less than 24 hours between the two flights.

This time is determined by the time difference between the arrival point of the first flight and the departure point of the second flight. However, this rule may vary due to the practices of local authorities.

Online check-in ends 90 minutes before the scheduled flight departure time for domestic and international flights. These times may vary due to the practices of local authorities. Click

[here](https://ajet.com/checkin/airports-online-checkin-status)for detailed information.When completing online check-in, the information you enter must match the details on your travel documents.

Once your online check-in is complete, don’t forget to download your boarding pass from our website or mobile app. The process of obtaining a boarding pass may vary based on local regulations. For detailed information, click

[here](https://ajet.com/checkin/airports-online-checkin-status)

**Self Check-in Kiosk**

Self-check-in kiosks are equipped with simple, touchscreen menus, allowing you to start the check-in process by verifying your identity using an ID number, e-ticket number, or PNR code.

Through self-check-in, you can check in for yourself and other passengers under the same booking and select your seats.

If you are traveling without baggage and have a connecting or return flight on the same day, you can also check in for those flights if there are less than 24 hours between them. Passengers without baggage can go straight to the boarding gate with their boarding passes after completing the check-in process.

Passengers with baggage can obtain their baggage tags at the self-check-in kiosks or self-bag drop machines and drop off their baggage at the self-bag drop station before proceeding to the gate. Excess baggage payments can also be made at the kiosks.


**Self Check-in Stations:**

Self-check-in is available at Istanbul Sabiha Gökçen, Ankara, Izmir, and Çukurova Airports.**3. Airport Check-in**

It is recommended to arrive at the airport at least 2 hour before domestic flights and 3 hours before international flights.

Check-in closes 45 minutes before domestic flights and 60 minutes before international flights. Check-in closure times may be earlier at some airports, so we recommend checking the check-in times at your departure airport. You must have your boarding pass and check-in baggage completed before this time.

If you have a layover of more than 72 hours at any point, you must reconfirm your next flight reservation; otherwise, it may be canceled.

For all domestic and international flights, you must be at the boarding gate no later than 20 minutes before departure. Please note that it can take up to 25 minutes to reach the gate at some terminals.

Check-in at the airport for flights departing from Algiers (ALG) starts 3 hours 30 minutes before the flight and ends 1 hour 30 minutes before departure. You must complete your check-in process between these times.


**Travel Documents****Domestic Travel:**

For domestic travel, you can use one of the following documents as identification:

National ID card

Temporary national ID certificate

Driver’s license

Passport and/or equivalent travel documents

Marriage certificate

Birth certificate (if presented with a parent’s ID within one month of birth)

Official documents with a photo, seal, and Turkish ID number issued by a government agency


You can complete the check-in process by presenting any of these documents.

**International Travel:**

For international travel, you can use the original passport or equivalent travel documents. Be sure to check the visa requirements of the country you are visiting before planning your trip.

Countries’ visa policies may change over time. We recommend checking the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or embassy websites for up-to-date visa information before your trip.**Passport**

A valid passport is required for international travel. If you are applying for a passport for the first time or renewing an expired passport, please note that it may take several days to complete.

You can find the necessary documents and schedule an appointment for a passport application on the Turkish Ministry of Interior’s Passport Services page.**Visa and Other Documents**

Countries’ visa policies may change over time. Therefore, we recommend checking the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and embassy websites to learn about the visa policies of the country you plan to visit.**Private Security Information (APIS)**

Border authorities may request personal information, called API or APIS (Advanced Passenger Information System), from the airline before you board the flight.

To avoid delays at the check-in counter and to use check-in options outside the counter, you must provide this information during your reservation.

The required APIS information includes:

Full name (as written in the passport)

Date of birth

Gender

Nationality

Passport number

Passport expiration date

Passport issuing country

Country of residence


**How is APIS Information Shared?**

We only share the APIS information you provide with the relevant authorities of the country you are traveling to. We do not share this information with any other person or organization.**How Can APIS Information Be Added?**

You can add APIS information to your purchased tickets by visiting the [Flight Management](https://ajet.com/en) section on our website.

According to new regulations, children can no longer travel on their parent’s passport. Children must have their passports, and visa applications must also be submitted for them.**Important Notices:**

Check your passport for any damage. Ensure the passport book is not detached or partially detached from its cover, and the plastic film on the photo page is not peeling or torn.

Make sure your passport has a blank page for the entry-exit stamp during international travel.

Ensure that the information on your travel documents (passport, ID, and ticket) is the same. In case of name changes, you must renew your passport or present an official document (marriage certificate, divorce decree, etc.) to the staff during the trip.

If you have a valid visa in an expired passport, please contact the consulate of the country you plan to visit.

The passport validity period required for travel varies by country. Ensure your passport’s validity is sufficient and that there is no damage (tears, scratches, etc.) to the passport. The signature section must also be signed by the passenger.

If you have a connecting flight, check whether a transit visa is required for the country where the connection will take place and whether there are any exemption conditions for visa-free transit.

Some Arab countries may deny entry if there is a valid or expired Israeli visa or entry-exit stamp in the passport.

You can travel without a passport to Ukraine, Moldova, Georgia, Azerbaijan, and Northern Cyprus with a new-style Turkish ID card. Child passengers must also have a new-style photo ID card. Temporary identity certificates are not accepted.

Additional documents (such as consent forms) may be required if an infant or child passenger is traveling with only one parent.
