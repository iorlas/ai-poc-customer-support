## Excess Baggage

### Excess Baggage

Our passengers are given certain allowance for the baggage that can be carried in the cargo compartment of the aircraft during flights. As for the international flights, the baggage allowance varies by the flight package and the route of flight. You can find the package contents of our domestic and international flights [here](https://ajet.com/en/corporate/rules-and-conditions/fare-notes).

You can find the information on free baggage allowance for domestic flights in [our baggage menu](https://ajet.com/en/corporate/rules-and-conditions/baggage).

Our passengers who have excess baggage can buy excess baggage allowance on AJet website or mobile application without having to go to the airport. You can visit our [services page](https://ajet.com/en/services/excess-baggage) to buy excess baggage.

The excess baggage fares are shown in the following table.

Excess Baggage Service Fee purchased outside the package can be refunded and exchanged until flight time if not used.

### Excess Baggage Fare for AJet International Flights

Passengers with excess baggage can purchase excess baggage allowance at the ticketing stage from the AJet website or mobile application without going to the airport. Excess baggage fees are available [here](https://cdn.cloud.ajet.com/prod/others/xbag_country_listWEBOUTSIDE_EN.pdf).

On the international-to-international connecting AJet flights, the total fare is obtained by calculating the excess baggage fare of each flight separately.

In excess baggage charges, excess baggage advantage is offered to passengers who do not have baggage allowance (basic package) on international flights with fixed fees up to 20 kg.

### General Rules

You can purchase excess baggage allowance from

[AJet.com](http://ajet.com)and AJet Mobile application until the check-in process closes.If the flight is changed against the passenger’s will, the purchased excess baggage allowance is not transferred to the new flight automatically. It is necessary contact the relevant call center to transfer the purchased excess baggage allowance to the new flight, or refund them.

If the baggage weight provided for the flight is lower than the purchased excess baggage allowance, the unused baggage allowance is not refunded. If the registered baggage weight provided for the flight is higher than the purchased excess baggage allowance, it is mandatory to pay the excess baggage fare corresponding to the weight difference.

In connecting flights, no refund will be made for the excess baggage allowance purchased, even if it is used in part of the flight and not used in the rest of the flight.

Excess baggage allowance cannot be purchased for infant passenger.

One piece of baggage may not be heavier than 32 kg.

The purchased excess baggage allowance may not be transferred to the 3rd persons.

The EMD (Electronic Miscellaneous Document) number is the document issued for additional services, such as baggage, seat selection, etc. that you have purchased. You can view your additional service invoice with this number

[here](https://ebiletfatura.ajet.com/).AJet reserves its right to change the excess baggage rules and conditions.
