## Meal Selection

Meal selection for our international flights can be made via ajet.com and AJet Mobile app, call center or agencies at the ticketing stage and after ticketing up to 24 hours before the flight time. In cases where there is 24 hours or less before the flight time, meal selection sales are not available.

The purchased meal selection can be refunded or exchanged for a different menu/meal through the call center up to 24 hours before the flight (including ticket cancellation, ticket change, etc.) Within 24 hours or less before the flight, as the menu/meal loading processes for flights begin, there is no right to a refund or change.

If a change is made to a reservation for which a menu/meal has been purchased, the purchased menu/meal information will be transferred if the new flight is available for menu/meal loading. If the modified flight is not available for menu/meal loading, the fee paid for the menu/meal will be refunded if there is more than 24 hours until the flight but will not be refunded if there is less than 24 hours.

Due to food safety, meal selection may not be offered for certain routes.

For operational reasons, menu/meal loading may not occur on some flights, in such cases, a refund for the meal selection fee can be requested by the passenger through the call center and feedback form.

Issues encountered with the purchased menu/meal during the flight should be reported to the cabin crew. Issues not reported to the cabin crew will not be accepted as complaints.

Passengers whose flight is canceled or changed due to airline-related issues can request a refund for the meal selection fee through the call center or feedback form.

The right to a refund is valid for 1 year from the date the menu/meal was purchased, according to the above rules.

There is an additional 10% tax for flights departing from Iran.
