# Sports Equipment

During your flights with AJet, you can benefit from the sports equipment transportation service, which you can purchase from agencies and call centers, for a fee.

Passengers with sport equipment can purchase sport equipment allowance from the AJet website or [AJet Mobile](https://ajetcom.page.link/3Xou) application during and after ticketing without going to the airport.

Please [click here](https://ajet.com/en/corporate/rules-and-conditions/sports-equipment) for more information about sport equipment.

Sports equipment is not considered within the scope of Free Baggage Allowance. Each equipment is charged according to the Sports Equipment Fee Tariff. Sports equipment can be carried in the cabin or in the luggage compartment of the aircraft for a fee, depending on weight or size.
