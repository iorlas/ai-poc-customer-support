## CIP Lounge

**General Rules**

These are the rules and conditions applicable to Miles&Smiles cardholders traveling on AJet flights during the general or paid use of CIP lounges.

CIP is a service offered only to Miles&Smiles member passengers.


For the passenger who is not a Miles&Smiles member, the passenger will be entitled to use for a fee after filling out the online membership form and creating a membership.


Passengers who are not Miles&Smiles members may be sold access to the CIP lounge, but Miles&Smiles membership must be completed prior to entry for lounge use.


Miles&Smiles Elite, Miles&Smiles Elite Plus, or Miles&Smiles Elite Corporate cardholder(s) are entitled to use the lounge free of charge. Additionally, a family member or a guest accompanying the passenger is entitled to use it free of charge. Family: Spouse and children under the age of 25 traveling with the passenger who is entitled to use the lounge. It is obligatory to enter the lounge together with the right holder.


Miles&Smiles Classic or Miles&Smiles Classic Plus cardholder(s) will be able to use the lounge for a fee.

**Other Rules**

Passengers who purchase the lounge usage service for a fee will only use the Miles&Smiles section of the lounge in the domestic private passenger lounges, which consist of two separate sections.


The implementation may be suspended during peak periods such as official holidays, before and after holidays.


Except for Sabiha Gökçen Airport, passengers who have purchased domestic lounge usage service can enter the lounge at least 2 hours before their flight.


Passengers who have purchased lounge usage service for Sabiha Gökçen Airport Domestic CIP Lounge can enter the lounge at the earliest 3 hours before their flight if the starting point of their flight is Sabiha Gökçen Airport, and at the earliest 4 hours before their flight if the starting point of their flight is not Sabiha Gökçen Airport, and their flight is a connecting flight.


Except for the Sabiha Gökçen International Airport Domestic Private Lounge, passengers who purchase lounge access but do not enter the lounge may request a refund under the right of withdrawal up to 3 hours before their flight. Refunds can be processed through the agency or call center.


In cases not caused by the passenger and due to operational reasons, a refund will be provided by applying to the customer relations directorate through

[https://ajet.com/en/corporate/feedback-form](https://ajet.com/en/corporate/feedback-form). No refunds will be issued in other cases.

There will be no refund in case of using the lounge service. (Exceptionally; in case of flight cancellations, the fee can be refunded even if the passenger has used the lounge).


In the event that the passenger requests a refund/change of the ticket before the flight takes place after using the lounges by paying the fee or with the rights provided by the Turkish Airlines business class ticket and/or status card, the transaction will be made after the lounge usage fee is deducted from the ticket price.


Even if the passenger traveling on a charter flight has a Turkish Airlines business class ticket and status card, they do not have the right to use the lounge. Exceptionally, passengers may have the right to use the lounge if the agency making a charter agreement purchases the exclusive use of the lounge for its passengers.


If the passenger who will travel with AJet flight has the right to accept a guest, the guest passenger must also be traveling on AJet flight. In this case, if the guest of the passenger traveling on an AJet flight is a passenger traveling on Turkish Airlines flight, free entrance to the lounge is not accepted.
