You can use credit card and debit card¹ (Visa, Visa Electron, MasterCard, Maestro, Diners Club, Discover, Troy), Klarna, Klarna online bank transfer, IDEAL, UnionPay payment methods.

You can make change and refund on your ticket from AJet.com, AJet Mobile and Call Center. You can make changes in state of emergency situations (flight cancellation, mandatory changes due to weather, etc.) on AJet.com and AJet Mobile application.

AJet offers special discounts for student, soldier, veteran/widowed/ martyr family and disabled passengers. In order for our passengers to benefit from this discount, they must submit the necessary documents before the flight. Please check our Special Passenger Discount page for detailed information about discounts.

Airlines may overbook seats on their flights in order to reduce the impact of no-shows (where a passenger fails to board a flight despite having a confirmed seat or ticket) and to allow passengers to travel on their preferred flights. Although every effort is made to ensure the travel of passengers with confirmed reservations, seat availability may not be guaranteed.

Unused tickets are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase, while used tickets are valid for 12 months from the date of the first flight. Refund requests can be made within 24 months from the date the ticket was issued. These periods may vary for tickets purchased at special fares. For detailed information, please see our Fare Notes page.

Cancelation or refund entitlements vary according to a fare's conditions. Visit our Fare Notes page to find out if you are entitled to a cancelation and refund on your ticket.

For tickets with more than one flight (round-trip, connecting or multiple), flights must be used in sequence. If you do not participate in any of the flights, all of the remaining flights (including return routes) will be automatically canceled if not processed by calling our call center.

With ticket number, you can view the invoice for the ticket you have purchased by clicking on the Financial e-Ticket heading on our website.

If you purchased your ticket from an agency, please contact the agency to receive your invoice.
