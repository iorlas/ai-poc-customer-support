## Sports Equipment

You can take your sports and camping equipment in the table below with you on your AJet flights. The transportation fees are determined in the table.

Sports equipment is not considered within the scope of Free Baggage Allowance. Each equipment is charged according to the Sports Equipment Fee Tariff. Sports equipment can be carried in the cabin or in the luggage compartment of the aircraft for a fee, depending on weight or size.

Sport equipment must be properly packaged in a hard, protective case designed to prevent damage. The airlines will not be liable for any loss or damage that may occur during transport for products transported in a soft, non-protective case despite this prohibition.

If the sports equipment is not suitable for the type of aircraft or is not properly packed in the appropriate dimensions, the equipment will be transported by cargo flights. The purchased sports equipment service fee can be refunded and changed until the flight time.

Passengers with sports equipment can purchase sports equipment from AJet website or [AJet Mobile](https://ajetcom.page.link/3Xou) application during and after ticketing without going to the airport.

You can visit our [services page](https://ajet.com/en/services/sports-equipment) to buy Sports Equipment.

**Winter Sports Equipment (Ski/Snowboard)**

One set includes one pair of skis or snowboard, one pair of ski poles, boots, and one helmet. The total weight must not exceed 23 kg.

**Golf Equipment**

One set includes golf clubs, golf balls, one pair of golf shoes, a tee set, and a golf bag. The total weight must not exceed 23 kg.

**Diving Equipment**

A diving set includes a wetsuit, buoyancy control device (BCD), mask, fins, flashlight, knife, snorkel, speargun, diving boots, diving tank, and regulator. Diving tanks must be empty. Underwater flashlights and similar tools can be carried in hand or checked baggage. To prevent any unexpected situation during the flight, the heat-producing part or the power source must be removed and packed to avoid activation. Removed batteries must be protected against short circuits. Spearguns must not be loaded and their tips must be packed separately.

### Water Ski, Surfboard, Rafting, Inflatable Boat Equipment

Includes one pair of standard water skis or one slalom ski (wakeboard). A surfboard must not exceed 292 cm (115 inches) in length and 60 cm (24 inches) in width.

### Parachute, Paraglider, Hang-Glider Equipment

Reservation is required. These items must be packed in protective bags and cannot contain personal items.

### Hockey, Ice Hockey, Lacrosse Equipment

Includes a helmet, a bag, one pair of skates, one stick, a ball (hockey or lacrosse), and protective gear (shoulder, elbow, knee pads, etc.). The total weight must not exceed 32 kg.

### Canoe

Includes one canoe and one set of paddles. The canoe must not exceed 3.5 meters in length. Motors or electric parts are not allowed.

### Bicycle

Bicycles must be packed in a strong cardboard box or special case. Motorized or electric bicycles cannot be transported in the cabin or checked baggage. To reduce size and help with loading, handlebars must be folded, seat lowered, pedals removed, and all protruding parts taken off. Reservation is required and the “BIKE” code must be added to the booking or check-in record.

### Archery Equipment

Includes a special case, bow, and arrows.

### Equestrian Equipment

Includes riding clothes, gloves, shoes, and a helmet. Total weight must not exceed 23 kg.

### Fishing Equipment

Includes a fishing rod, reel, net, one pair of fishing boots, and a tackle bag.

### Hunting Equipment

Includes hunting knives, all types of non-rifled hunting guns, pump-action shotguns, and similar tools.

### Mountaineering Equipment

Includes a special protective bag, one pair of poles, one ice axe, and one pair of mountaineering boots or shoes. Items classified as dangerous goods are not allowed. A reservation is required. Cargo services carry oversized items.

### Windsurfing Equipment

Includes one board, one boom, one mast, and one sail. Maximum board length is 292 cm (115 inches), and width is 60 cm (24 inches). The mast and sail must be removable and foldable.

### Camping Equipment

Includes a tent, metal rods, and related parts.

### Bowling Equipment

Includes one bowling ball, one pair of bowling shoes, and a bowling bag.

### Javelin Equipment

Includes a javelin and a javelin stand (fixed or wheeled). Total weight must not exceed 23 kg.

**Tennis Equipment**

Includes tennis balls, a racket, tennis clothes, and shoes. Total weight must not exceed 23 kg.

IATA rules and conditions will be applied for other sports equipment which are not listed above.
