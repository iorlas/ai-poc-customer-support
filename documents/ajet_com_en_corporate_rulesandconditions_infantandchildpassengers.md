## Infant and Child Passengers

### Infant and Child Passengers

**Infant Passenger**

Infant passengers are the ones who are 0 to 2 years old and have not celebrated their second birthday.

Infant passengers are not admitted to our flights within the first 48 hours of their births.

Two- to seven-day-old babies are admitted to the flights with a doctor's report stating that "There is no inconvenience in his or her traveling by air."

A baby’s ticket is issued for the infant passenger. The infant passenger does not have a seat.

The infant passenger can only travel with one of the parents or a companion. The non-parent companion must be at least 18 years old.

Additional documents (consent) may be requested for international flights of Infants passengers traveling with their parents (any of them).

Infant passengers are admitted to all the cabins in the plane.

Infant passengers travel on the lap of their accompanying adult with an additional safety belt.

Upon request, infant passengers may travel in a seat. In this case, an infant ticket must be issued and a separate seat must be purchased.

For infants traveling in a seat, a car-type infant seat or child seat must be provided

**by the passenger.**Each accompanying adult may accompany one infant. If traveling with more than one infant: The first infant may fly with an infant ticket and travel on the lap. An infant ticket must also be issued for the second infant, and a separate seat must be purchased. For the second infant to travel safely in a seat and be secured with a seatbelt, a car-type infant seat or child seat must be provided

**by the passenger.**We do not provide a companion flight attendant for infant passengers.

It is recommended to use a Stroller Protection Bag to protect strollers during the process of sending them to the aircraft hold. The bag can be purchased at AJet counters or AJet ticket sales offices at the airport; please visit

[the Baggage page](https://ajet.com/en/corporate/rules-and-conditions/baggage)for pricing details.In countries where travel is allowed with the new type of Turkish ID card without a passport, infants passengers are also required to have a new type of Turkish ID card that includes a photo.

**Child Passenger**

Child passengers are the ones who have celebrated their second birthday but not yet their twelfth birthday up until the starting date of their travel.

A child’s ticket is issued for the child passenger.

The child passenger is provided with a seat like any other passenger.

Child passengers are admitted to all the cabins in the plane.

Children between 2 and 7 can only travel with one of their parents or a companion who is at least 18 years old.

Additional documents (consent) may be requested for international flights of child passengers traveling with their parents (any of them).

Child passengers between the ages of 7 and 11, who have celebrated their 7th birthday but not their 12th birthday on the day of travel, may travel alone with the permission of their parents or legal representatives, after signing the Authorization Certificate for the Unaccompanied Child Transportation.

All procedures can only be proceeded from counters at the airports for children’s passengers ages between 7 and 12.

In countries where travel is allowed with the new type of Turkish ID card without a passport, child passengers are also required to have a new type of Turkish ID card that includes a photo.


**Child passengers who travel alone:**

Our children's passengers who have celebrated their seventh birthday but have not celebrated their twelfth birthday can travel unaccompanied only if permitted by a parent or legal representative to travel alone.

All reservations must be approved in order for the child to travel unaccompanied.

Priority service is provided at all airports for the processing of child passengers who will travel unaccompanied.

Due to flight safety regulations, the number of unaccompanied minor services available on our flights is limited per flight, depending on the number of cabin crew members. Therefore, it is important to request unaccompanied minor services when purchasing your ticket.

Children traveling on connecting flights operated by AJet and/or another airline are not accepted unaccompanied by AJet. For reservation changes related to this issue, please contact the airline or travel agency where the flight reservation was made.

In order for the child passenger to travel unaccompanied, one of the parents or the legal representative will fill in and sign the

**‘Authorization Certificate for the Unaccompanied Child Transportation’**.With this document, the child will be handed over unaccompanied and delivered to the authorized person indicated in the document in return of his or her signature. If the person who delivers the child to the authorities at the airport is not the parent or a legal representative, a notarized authorization must be provided.
